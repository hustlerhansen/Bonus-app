# BONUSPLAY — Spill. Tjen. Opplev.

## Treatment
30 seconds, **9:16**, Norwegian Bokmål, adults 18+. Remember the playful progression from activities to points to simulated rewards. Navy #070b1c, blue #2f7bff, purple #9b4dff, gold #ffd24d; Unbounded display and DM Sans supporting type. Existing BONUSPLAY diamond icon is the hero. Coins, circular game targets, streak rings and the gift-card facet form matched transitions. Energy rises through the game, pauses for the reward, resolves on a gold brand field. All monetary rewards are visibly DEMO; no real payments or launch claims. One original electronic instrumental, no voiceover or SFX.
