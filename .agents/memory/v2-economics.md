BonusPoints represent a financial liability for BonusPlay. Every point must be explainable through a traceable ledger event. Do not assume one BP has a fixed monetary value.

**Why:** The user's V2 master specification makes financial integrity the highest priority and requires configurable economics rather than inheriting the demo's illustrative exchange rate.

**How to apply:** For V2, the commercial loop is partner payment for a measurable action, server-side verification, user points, reward redemption and retained margin. Revenue, liability, reward cost and margin must reconcile; registration/clicks alone cannot qualify referral rewards.
