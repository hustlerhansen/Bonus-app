==================================================
1. PRODUCT IDEA
==================================================

BONUSPLAY is a Norwegian rewards platform where users earn points and XP by completing activities.

Users can:

• log in daily
• build streaks
• complete daily missions
• play mini-games
• watch rewarded ads
• answer surveys
• complete sponsored offers
• participate in events
• compete on leaderboards
• unlock achievements
• level up
• invite friends
• earn points
• redeem points for rewards

For the MVP, all external providers and monetary rewards should operate in DEMO MODE.
